Ledger and lender desk

India UPI-debit orders unpaid after the link window go first to the passenger's backup credit card if they opted in; passengers with no card (or a declined card) go to the lender. Nothing about credit is shown to the passenger until the lender confirms. International card payers have no lender: their link stays open longer and, if it expires, the order shows as expired unpaid.
