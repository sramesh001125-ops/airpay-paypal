AirPay

Take the order with no network. Get paid when you land.

AirPay lets cabin crew record purchases offline, then settles each one over the cheapest PayPal-connected rail for the passenger's country, cutting card cost across PayPal's markets. India UPI debit is an add-on with a lender-backed pay-later fallback. Every order is screened first and logged in a tamper-evident audit trail.

Demo build for the PayPal AI Hackathon. Payments are simulated unless PayPal sandbox keys are configured. The fee table is illustrative and the lender and UPI-style legs are simulated.

What is it?

A payment layer for places with slow or no network, starting with in-flight sales. Orders are captured on a crew device, then settled after landing through PayPal's sandbox, using bank-based rails where they cost less than cards. International buyers pay by card, PayPal wallet or a local bank rail; India's UPI debit is an add-on.

What can I do?

Take offline orders within device limits for each payer type, see which rail is cheapest for each buyer and why, watch a single-use QR get burned only on verified payment, see the SHIELD pre-screen, run the India lender fallback, and check every step in the audit log.

Where next?

Real lender and bank partners, a live UPI-style interoperability leg, the full SHIELD service behind the adapter, more places than aircraft (trains, ships, remote areas), and PayPal's agent tools for checkout by conversation.

End to end, by phase

1

Order offline on board

Crew device captures items, buyer, amount and payment type with no connection. Per-order and per-device limits for that payment type apply on the device.

2

Route on landing

Orders sync once and pass the SHIELD pre-screen. An AI agent proposes the rail; a rule guard accepts or overrides it using a cost table and an irreversibility cap.

Open workspace
3

Pay by link or QR

The passenger pays before leaving. The QR burns only when a signed payment event confirms settlement.

Open workspace
4

India pay-later fallback (add-on)

For UPI-debit passengers only: if the savings-account payment is not made, the passenger's confirmed backup credit card is charged first (its own credit line, no loan). Passengers with no card (or a declined card) go to the lender, who settles the airline and books a short overdraft on the passenger's own savings account (flat charge, 7 days maximum, auto-debit). International card payers with a confirmed consent are charged the same way; without one they keep a longer link and have no overdraft.

5

Settle and audit

A hash-chained log and an audit agent check limits, rails, QR state, lender timing and amounts.
